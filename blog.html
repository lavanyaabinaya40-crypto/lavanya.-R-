My First Steps in Python

By Lavanya R, 1st-year B.E. CSE, SRM Valliammai Engineering College

🔦 Why I started

I joined B.E. Computer Science and Engineering with one big dream: to become a software developer. Every developer starts somewhere, so I chose Python as my first programming language.

💻 My very first program

Everyone's first program is the same. It prints a message on the screen:

print("Hello, World!")

One line of code, and the computer talked back to me. That small moment made me love coding.

📚 What I am learning

I am still at the beginning. I am learning the basics step by step: how to print output, how to store values in variables, and how to write simple programs. I am not an expert yet, and that is okay.

🚪 What is next

I want to practise every day, build my first small project, and share it here on my portfolio. I will keep writing about my journey as I learn.

Every expert was once a beginner in the dark. 🔦
